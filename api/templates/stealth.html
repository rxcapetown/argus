IN STEALTH

Argus Data Labs creates RL environments for training, and human judgment for data.

Agents are learning to do real work in every domain at once. We are generalists. We build the environments agents train in, and we supply the human judgment that tells them what good work looks like.

A script can check whether a task was completed. It cannot judge whether it was done well: whether the substitution was reasonable, the refund fair, the answer what the customer actually meant. That judgment comes from people who have done the work. It is the scarcest data in AI, and it is what we create.

WHAT WE CREATE

RL environments

Simulated worlds where agents practise real tasks and are scored on the outcome. Built for training, not demos.

Human judgment

Experts review, grade and correct agent work against written rubrics, and a second expert checks every grade before it becomes data.

Evals

Repeatable tests that show exactly where an agent fails: the end state it leaves behind, the rules it broke, the calls it got wrong. Categories run from coding, operations and legal to commerce and support.

GENERALIST

Generalist by design.

We do not build for one vertical. Our environments cover storefronts, travel bookings, package shipping and grocery ordering today, and extend to customer support, payments and office operations. Wherever agents will work, there is a training ground to build and a standard to set.
