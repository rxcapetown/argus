Get paid for your trade documents

Argus Data Labs pays $1,000 for every shipment dossier that passes verification. First 15 verified dossiers. Checking slots…

How it works

  1. Register below. You get a contributor code. Accept the paid terms (short, plain language).
  2. Send your documents. One complete shipment: commercial invoice, packing list, and bill of lading or airway bill at minimum. Upload here or email them. You review the redaction before anything is final.
  3. Annotate. Mark any exception against our fixed taxonomy (wrong HS code, value mismatch, quantity variance, and so on). No exception? That counts too.
  4. Verification. A different forwarder cross-checks your annotation. This is the step that releases payment.
  5. Paid. $1,000 within 14 days of passing verification.

What counts as verified

  • Complete document set for one shipment
  • Your annotation against the exception taxonomy
  • Confirmed cross-verification by a different forwarder

If verification fails, you can fix and resubmit once.

Your data, your control

  • You review every redaction before it is final
  • Names, addresses, and account numbers are stripped
  • Values and prices stay in: that is the signal
  • Only the redacted version enters the corpus

Questions

When do I get paid?
Within 14 days after your dossier passes verification. Verification means a complete document set, your annotation, and a confirmed cross-check by a different forwarder.

What if my dossier fails verification?
You get one fix-and-resubmit. After that the slot goes back to the pool.

How do I receive the money?
We arrange it directly with you after verification: bank transfer or Zelle for US contributors, wire for others.

Who sees my documents?
Only the redacted version is shared with the cross-verifier. Your company name never appears in the corpus.

Why only 15?
This is a paid pilot to price real acquisition cost. Contributors in the pilot get first priority when paid licensing begins.
