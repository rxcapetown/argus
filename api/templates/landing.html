How contributing works

  1. Upload — a commercial invoice, bill of lading, or packing list. List any names that must never appear.
  2. Review — side-by-side: your original next to the proposed redaction, every detected item boxed and listed.
  3. Approve — uncheck anything to restore it, then confirm. Approved items are permanently removed from the document — not covered up — and you get an audit log.

Names, addresses, contacts, B/L and container numbers never enter the corpus. Prices, quantities, weights, and charges stay — they're the signal.
